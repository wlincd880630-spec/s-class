状语从句 —— 让步 although

Although / Though + 从句，+ 主句。主句侧不再用 but 连接同一转折。

Although the sun was strong, everyone arrived on time.

Though I was afraid at first, I felt braver later.

语义：虽然 A，仍然 B（B 是重点）。

状语从句 —— 不连用红线

禁止叠用错误正确（二选一）
although + but Although it was cold, but we went out. Although it was cold, we went out.
It was cold, but we went out.
because + so Because he was tired, so he went to bed. Because he was tired, he went to bed.
He was tired, so he went to bed.
写作自检：句子里转折/因果只保留一层连词——从句引导词 或 并列连词，不要两套同时上场。

状语从句 —— 小结

  • 让步：Although/Though + 从句 + 主句；可与 but 版互转，不可同句叠用。
  • 不连用：× Although…, but… · × Because…, so…
  • 暑假计划可写：Although the plan may not be perfect, I will follow it.
  • 学习顺序：让步 → 例句 → 陷阱 → 红线 → 对话 → 快选 → 口语与写作。
  • 下一页：时间 until · as soon as + 合并句（Page 05）。
