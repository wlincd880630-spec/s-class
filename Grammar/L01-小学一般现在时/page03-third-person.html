👦👧🐱 He / She / It 加 s

主语是 He、She、It（第三人称单数）→ 动词要加 s 或 es
He / She / It + plays / reads / goes …

🔄 对比记忆

主语动词例句
I / We / TheyplayThey play football.
He / She / ItplaysShe plays football.
I / WereadWe read books.
He / ShereadsHe reads books.

✏️ 拼写规则

🌳 人物与动物例句

Tom runs fast.汤姆跑得很快。（Tom = He）
Amy likes dancing.艾米喜欢跳舞。（Amy = She）
The cat sleeps on the sofa.猫在沙发上睡觉。（cat = It）
My brother goes to school by bus.我哥哥坐公交车上学。
