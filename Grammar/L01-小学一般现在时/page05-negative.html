🚫 否定句 don't / doesn't

表示「不……」时，在动词前面加 don't 或 doesn't，后面的动词用原形。

📐 公式

I / You / We / They
+ don't + 动词原形
He / She / It
+ doesn't + 动词原形
She+ doesn't+ like（不是 likes！）

🔄 肯定 ↔ 否定

⚠️ 易错提醒

  • doesn't 后面动词不加 s：✓ She doesn't like milk. ✗ She doesn't likes milk.
  • have 的否定：He doesn't have a dog.（不是 doesn't has）
  • 缩写：do not = don't，does not = doesn't
I do not like rainy days. = I don't like rainy days.我不喜欢下雨天。
