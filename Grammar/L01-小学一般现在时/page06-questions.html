❓ 疑问句 Do / Does

把 Do 或 Does 放在句首，就变成一般疑问句。回答用 Yes/No + 简短回答。

📐 公式

Do + I/you/we/they + 动词原形 ?
Does + he/she/it + 动词原形 ?
Do+ you+ like?

💬 问答练习

🗣️ 简短回答

问句肯定回答否定回答
Do you like music? Yes, I do. No, I don't.
Does she read books? Yes, she does. No, she doesn't.
Do they play football? Yes, they do. No, they don't.
