🧩 连词成句（想一想）

every day / football / plays / He
He plays football every day.
books / read / We / after school
We read books after school.
like / don't / I / spicy food
I don't like spicy food.
Does / she / English / study / ?
Does she study English?

She doesn't watch TV at night.
Do you like your school? Yes, I do.
