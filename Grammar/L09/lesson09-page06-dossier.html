语篇中三处空缺须选 was drinking / found out / came across 等地道搭配。 警惕：find out、come across 表「察觉 / 偶遇」时为短暂动词，一般不用进行时（如 was finding out 为典型错配）。
CLASSIFIED FILE #404 — THE MISSING DIAMOND

Last Friday night, while Museum Agent Lin was updating the night log, she quietly at her desk. Then the alarm on Level 3 rang twice. She immediately that the “Star Blue” diamond in Hall B was missing. On the way to the back stairs, she a half-open toolbox — and a single black hair on its handle.

Slide 2 · 瞬间动词与「背景进行」

本卷宗 · 中考完形/阅读高频 find out（查明；发觉）

正： She immediately found out that the diamond was missing.

误： She was finding out that the diamond was missing.（把「获知结果」写成了拖长动作）

本卷宗 · 偶遇义 come across（偶然看见 / 碰到）

正： On the way, she came across a half-open toolbox.

误： She was coming across a toolbox.（偶遇为短促结果，不用进行体）

人教八年级 · 阅读常见 realize / notice / discover

I suddenly realized I was on the wrong bus.（顿悟，一点即明）

Did you notice the sign on the wall?（注意到，瞬间完成）

They discovered a secret door behind the bookshelf.（发现结果）

人教话题 · 突发声响 hear / see（表「听见一声 / 看见一幕」）

We heard someone shout “Help!” from the river.（听见呼喊，常作点事件）

She saw a dog rush into the street.（看见突发动作）

可与 was listening / was looking 对照：听/看的过程用进行，听到/看到结果多用过去式。

中考记叙文 · 叙事节奏 go out / ring / start / stop 等「突变」动词

The lights suddenly went out while we were discussing the plan.

The alarm rang twice while she was updating the log.

The rain stopped just as we were packing up the tent.（雨停，状态突变）

The film started while many people were still finding their seats.

叙事常用 · 获知与认出 learn / recognize / spot

We learned the news the next day.（得知结果，不宜 *was learning the news* 表「获知」）

She recognized his voice at once.（认出，瞬间完成）

A visitor spotted a crack in the glass case.（瞥见、发现，点事件）

点事件 · 到达与离开 arrive / enter / leave / reach

The team arrived while volunteers were setting up chairs.（到场落点常用过去式）

He entered the hall and waved to the crowd.（进入，短动作）

She reached the top step and paused.（到达某处，常作一点）

对照：was arriving 多强调「在途中」，写到「已经到了」叙述落点仍常用 arrived。

突发 · 状态翻转 happen / break out / turn off（表突变）

Something strange happened while everyone was sleeping.

An argument suddenly broke out near the exit.

Someone turned off the main switch; the hall went dark.（关掉，一下完成）

身体反应 · 短促动作 jump / fall / hit / nod / shake one's head

He jumped up when he heard his name called.（惊跳、站起，点动作）

A book fell off the shelf while she was dusting it.

She nodded and turned away.（点头、转身，叙述里常作相继短动词）

错配对照（改错 / 完形干扰项常见）

表「结果 / 获知 / 偶遇」时（不推荐）推荐写法
was finding out …found out …
was coming across …came across …
was realizing …realized …
was noticing …noticed …
was discovering …（表「发现结果」）discovered …
was learning …（表「得知」）learned / learnt …
was recognizing …recognized …
was spotting …spotted …
was arriving …（表「已经到场」）arrived …

小结：背景用 was / were + doing；突变、获知、偶遇用一般过去时或短暂动词落点，与教材和中考真题叙述习惯一致。
