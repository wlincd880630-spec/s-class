第12讲 · Page 07 · 背诵讲义

对齐 Page01–06：图书馆 · that · 转述与时态后退 · wh-/if · It is said/reported · 被动中的宾从 · 综测自检

① 三要素速记卡（全面版）

宾语从句做题三问：①缺什么信息→选引导词；②从句是否「陈述语序」；③主从时态是否呼应（+ 是否客观真理）。下面按「要素 → 规则 → 正误对照」铺开，可与 Page02–04 课堂页交叉背诵。

要素一 · 引导词

按「从句里缺不缺成分、缺哪一类信息」选词，而不是按中文句尾有没有问号。

  • that：从句本身是完整陈述（主谓宾齐全），只「打包」进主句。think / believe / know / hope / say / agree / hear / see / find / show… + (that) + 主 + 谓。that 常可省，但两个宾从并列时，第二个 that 建议保留以便看清层次。
  • wh-：从句里缺一个信息位——what（物/内容）who（人，作主语）whose（谁的）which（哪一个）where / when / why / how…；how long / how often / how far / how soon 等也属 wh- 类。
  • if / whether：对应中文「是否」，从句仍是陈述语序。whether … or not、介词后（depend on / talk about）、whether to do、动词 discuss（不再加 about）等 → 优先 whether。
  • that ≠ whether：He said that he was tired.（陈述）≠ He asked if / whether he was tired.（疑问意义「是否」）
  • what ≠ that：缺「说的内容/事情」用 what：I don’t know what he said.；已知是陈述事实用 that：I know that he is honest.
要素二 · 陈述语序

从句内部 = 「引导词 + 主语 + 谓语 + 其余」，绝不再写成一般疑问倒装。

  • 外壳动词：Could you tell me… / Do you know… / I wonder… / Please tell me… / Would you mind telling me… + 宾从。
  • 正：Could you tell me where the station is? / … how I can get there? / … whether the ticket office is still open?
  • 错：where is the station / how can I get there / whether is the ticket office open（错在疑问语序）。
  • wh- 在从句中作主语时，语序本来就「像陈述」：Could you tell me who will help us? / I don’t know what happened.（不要写成 who will help us 以外的倒装）
  • 插入语、否定仍放在主语前后正常位置：I don’t know why he didn’t come.
要素三 · 时态呼应

看主句谓语时间 + 从句描述的是不是「永远对的规律」。

  • 主句现在（He says / thinks…）：从句按实际时间选时态，不必为「呼应」硬改：He says (that) he is busy. / He says (that) he will come.
  • 主句过去（said / told / asked…）：从句谓语常退一步（详见 ② 表）：He said (that) he was busy. / She said (that) she would help me.
  • 客观真理 / 谚语 / 相对不变规律：转述时常仍用现在时：The teacher said (that) the earth goes round the sun.
  • 与状语从句区分：「主将从现」多出现在 if / when 引导的状语从句里；宾语从句里 if 常表「是否」，时态按上面三条处理。
高频搭配 · say / tell / ask
  • say：say (that)… / say to sb (that)…；不说 say me that…。
  • tell：tell sb (that)… / tell sb wh-…；须带人作宾语：Tell me whether…
  • ask：ask sb if / whether… / ask sb wh-…；ask if 表「是否」，不是「如果」（条件状语另讲）。
报道壳 · It is … that（与本讲 P05 衔接）
  • It is said / reported / believed / hoped… that + 陈述从句；that 在此类结构中不要省，避免与形式主语结构混淆。
  • 辨错：不说 It hopes that… → 用 It is hoped that… 或 People hope that…。

② 时态后退（转述）扩展表

前提：主句谓语为过去时（said / told / asked…），宾语从句常「退一步」；若主句是现在时（He says…），从句时态多保持原样。下列为中考高频对照。

③ 实用语料库（口语 · 写作 · 本讲话题）

带标签的句子可直接搬进「问路 / 请求」「书信与议论文首段」「活动报道」。建议挑 8～10 句抄在积累本上，写作时改人名、时间与地点即可复用。

口语请求 · 澄清 · 跟进

比单个单词回答更自然，适合对话与听力复述。

  • Could you tell me where I can charge my phone?
  • I’m not sure whether I should go first or wait in line.
  • I wonder how long the meeting will take.
  • I was wondering if the last bus still runs at 10:30.
  • Sorry, I didn’t catch what you said just now.
口语观点 · 感受（随口表达）

接 that 从句，语气比 I think 更柔和或更肯定。

  • I feel that teamwork really matters in a project.
  • I guess that we’d better start a little earlier.
  • I’m afraid that I can’t make it tonight.
  • To be honest, I doubt whether this plan will work without more practice.
写作开篇 · 观点（议论文/邮件）

放在首段点题，后接 For example / First… 展开。

  • Many students believe that reading opens a bigger world.
  • There is no doubt that good habits shape our daily life.
  • Personally, I believe that balance between study and rest is important.
  • It is clear that everyone needs a safe environment at school.
写作原因 · 结果 · 建议（主体段）

that 引导说明理由，衔接自然。

  • The main reason is that we lack enough sleep during exam weeks.
  • I realized that small steps lead to big progress.
  • Teachers suggest that we should review notes every weekend.
  • Parents hope that we can manage screen time by ourselves.
图书馆 · 学习 · P01–P02
  • I hear that Harry Potter is very popular.
  • We hope that every student has a library card.
  • The librarian said that I had to return the book on time.
  • I find that taking notes helps me remember key grammar.
  • Could you show me how I can search for books on the computer?
礼貌询问 · 出行 · P04
  • Could you tell me whether I need to print the form first?
  • I don’t know if the volunteers work in shifts.
  • Please tell me how I can renew the book online.
  • I’d like to know which gate we should wait at.
  • Excuse me, could you tell me if this seat is free?
报道口吻 · 通知 · P05
  • It is said that phones must be kept silent at school.
  • It is believed that short breaks help our eyes.
  • It was reported that the sports meet would be held next Friday.
  • It has been announced that the library will close at 6 pm on Fridays.
被动 + 宾从 · 校会 / 志愿 · P05
  • We were told that safety comes first.
  • Tom was asked whether he could help at the station.
  • Students were reminded that they should wear helmets when cycling.
写作环保 · 健康 · 科技（八九年级话题）

适合「倡议书 / 演讲稿 / 读后续写」中的价值句。

  • Scientists believe that cutting down waste starts with daily choices.
  • We all agree that walking to school is good for our health.
  • Some people worry that AI tools may make students less independent.
  • I learned that saving water at home is easier than I thought.
口语结束语 · 礼貌收尾
  • I really appreciate that you took the time to explain it to me.
  • I promise that I will double-check the time tomorrow.
  • Let me know if you need any more information.
④ 易错红线 · 课堂速改（8 条）
  1. Could you tell me when does it start? → when it starts
  2. I wonder if is it true. → if it is true
  3. He said if he was tired. → that he was tired
  4. It depends on if we win. → on whether we win
  5. She said she will come. → she would come
  6. I don’t know what he will come. → if/whether he will come
  7. We discussed about whether … → discussed whether …
  8. He told that … → He told me that …（tell 常带宾语）
⑤ Page 06 综测前 · 自检清单
  1. 先判断从句缺「事实 / 信息 / 是否」哪一类，再选引导词。
  2. 见到 Could you tell me，默认检查陈述语序。
  3. 主句过去时，从句谓语是否与「后退表」一致。
  4. 谚语、科学事实、地理事实：优先考虑现在时不变。
  5. whether … or not、介词后、不定式前 → 优先 whether。
  6. 辨错题：标出引导词、语序、时态、介词搭配四处。
  7. 语篇题：先抓每段首句，再找题干关键词定位。
  8. 写作迁移：一句观点 + that 从句展开，比堆砌短句更得分。
