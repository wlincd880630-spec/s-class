侦探线索

四个场景里，am / is / are 回到过去时常变成 was / was / were。下面先读一句「现在 vs 昨天」的对比，再判断哪一条概括最准确。

对比

The weather is sunny today. But it was cloudy yesterday.

选最概括的一项。

学生练习 · 句型转换

把 Now 句改成 Past（注意 am / is / are → was / were）。先自己写，再点「看参考答案」核对。

NowI am in the art room.
PastI was in the art room.
NowThe mountains are high.
PastThe mountains were high.
NowThe beach is beautiful.
PastThe beach was beautiful.
NowYou are in the library.
PastYou were in the library.
NowIt is cold today.
PastIt was cold yesterday.
NowMy friends are excited.
PastMy friends were excited.
