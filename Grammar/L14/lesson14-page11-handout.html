工坊探秘社 · L14

Passive Voice · 被动语态

第14讲 · 被动语态 · 背诵讲义

be + 过去分词 · 现在/过去/情态/将来/完成 · 打印 A4

使用方法：① 背公式与口诀 ② 熟读四则故事（工坊 / 发明馆 / 规则辩论 / 校园计划）③ 完成默写线 ④ 对照易错自改。

① 构成 · be + 过去分词

主语+ be (am/is/are/was/were)+ 过去分词 (done)

拓展记忆：情态/将来被动里的 be 用原形（should be / will be）；现在完成被动里的 been 是 be 的过去分词（has been / have been）。

时态结构例句
一般现在am/is/are + done Sky lanterns are made of bamboo.
一般过去was/were + done The telephone was invented in 1876.
情态情态 + be + done Teens should be allowed to choose clothes.
一般将来will be + done The bridge will be built next year.
现在完成have/has been + done Homework has been finished.

红线：被动 = be + 过去分词，不是原形，也不是 V-ing。✗ is make → ✓ is made；✗ will built → ✓ will be built；✗ has finished（被动）→ ✓ has been finished

② 意义 · 承受者作主语

强调动作落在谁身上、结果、地点或规则；施事者（谁做的）常省略，确需说明时用 by 引出。

  • They make phones. → Phones are made.（主动 ↔ 被动）
  • They are made in China.（强调产地）
  • The air is badly polluted.

③ 主动 → 被动 · 四步

  1. 找出主动句宾语（承受者）
  2. 宾语 → 被动句主语
  3. 动词 → be + 过去分词（be 随新主语变化）
  4. 原主语 → by + … 或省略

本四步先用于一般现在/过去被动；遇到情态、将来、完成时，要保留对应结构：should be / will be / has been + done。

主动被动
They make kites by hand. Kites are made by hand.
George Crum invented potato chips. Potato chips were invented by George Crum.
Bell invented the telephone. The telephone was invented by Bell.

④ by 短语 · 何时省略

  • The telephone was invented by Bell.（指明发明者）
  • This ring was made in Thailand.（产地 → in，无 by）
  • Kites are made by hand.（by hand 表方式：手工制作，不是“由手发明”）

⑤ 过去时被动 · was / were + done

问时间：When was … invented? — It was invented in …

  • When was the telephone invented?
  • Potato chips were invented by mistake in 1853.
  • The telephone was invented in 1876.
  • Basketball was invented by James Naismith.

⑥ 情态被动 · should be allowed to

  • Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes.
  • Sixteen-year-olds should not be allowed to drive.
  • Should teens be allowed to work on weekends?

⑦ made of / from / in

介词用法例句
made of 看得出原材料Shirts are made of cotton.
made from 形态变化明显Wine is made from grapes.
made in 产地/地点They are made in China.

⑧ 一般将来被动 · will be + done

问时间：When will … be …? — It will be … next year / in September.

  • The bridge will be built next year.
  • More trees will be planted next spring.
  • The work won't be finished today.
  • When will the new school be opened?

⑨ 现在完成被动 · have/has been + done

already / yet：强调与现在有联系的结果。

  • Homework has been finished.
  • The work has not been finished yet.
  • Has the classroom been cleaned yet?
  • Many trees have been planted this year.

⑩ 易错红线

  • 漏 be：✗ The tea grown → ✓ The tea is grown
  • be 与主语不一致：✗ Shirts is made → ✓ Shirts are made
  • 用原形/过去式：✗ is make → ✓ is made
  • made of China → ✓ made in China
  • 情态后漏 be：✗ should allowed → ✓ should be allowed
  • 完成被动漏 been：✗ has finished（被动）→ ✓ has been finished
  • 将来被动漏 be：✗ will built → ✓ will be built

⑪ 默写线

看中文写英文；写不出先看本页公式，再回 Page 02～03 复习构成与转换，回 Page 12 复习将来/完成。

1. 天灯由竹子和纸制成。

2. 薯片是偶然发明的。

3. 电话于 1876 年被发明。

4. 青少年应被允许选自己的衣服。

5. 大城市空气被严重污染。

6. 桥将于明年被建好。

7. 作业已经被做完。

8. 这些风筝是在潍坊手工制作的。

9. 这个工作今天不会被完成。

⑫ 口诀

承受者作主语，be 随主语变，动词用过去分词；by 表施事可省略；情态后接 be done；将来 will be done；完成 have/has been done。

背诵出口：能默写 5 个基础句 + 2 个拓展句；能说清 of/from/in 与 by 的区别；能指出 will be 与 has been 中的 be/been 不能省。

故事 A · 传统手工艺

  1. Sky lanterns are made of bamboo and covered with paper.天灯由竹子和纸制成。
  2. Paper is folded before it is cut with scissors.纸先被折好，再被剪出图案。
  3. Clay is shaped by hand and fired at high heat.泥塑被手工塑形后高温烧制。

故事 B · 发明馆

  1. When was the telephone invented? It was invented by Alexander Graham Bell in 1876.电话何时被发明？1876年由贝尔发明。
  2. Potato chips were invented by mistake in 1853.薯片于1853年偶然发明。
  3. Printing was invented in China long ago.印刷术很早在中国发明。

故事 C · 规则辩论

  1. Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes.青少年应被允许选自己的衣服。
  2. I do not think sixteen-year-olds should be allowed to drive.我认为16岁少年不应被允许开车。
  3. Peter should be allowed to take the test later.应允许彼得晚些参加考试。

故事 D · 校园计划与完成

  1. The bridge will be built next year. More trees will be planted next spring.桥明年将被建好；明年春天将种更多树。
  2. Homework has been finished. The letter has already been sent.作业已做完；信已经寄出。
  3. Many trees have been planted this year. Has the classroom been cleaned yet?今年已种许多树；教室打扫了吗？
