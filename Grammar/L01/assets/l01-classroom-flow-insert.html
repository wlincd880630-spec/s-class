第 1 页

动作义与状态义动词

课堂流程

  1. 听老师讲解「动作家族 / 状态家族」。

第 4 页

状态义：否定与疑问

课堂流程

  1. 学习否定缩写：isn't / aren't / am not。
  2. 陈述句变一般疑问；练习 Yes/No 简短回答。
  3. 选择疑问句：or 连接两选项。

讲义填空

1. It is cold. → It cold.

2. They are apples. → They apples.

3. I am tired. → I tired.

4. This room is tidy. → this room tidy?

5. Are they apples? — Yes, they . / No, they .

6. Is he from the US? — Yes, he . / No, he .

7. Would you like tea coffee?

8. Are you coming by bus on foot?

9. 选择疑问语调： 升， 降。

第 5 页

第三人称单数

课堂流程

  1. 对比 I/We/You/They 与 He/She/单数主语动词形式。
  2. 归纳四规则：+s / +es / y→ies / have→has。
  3. 限时操练，巩固变形。

讲义填空

1. 一般情况加 ：play → he

2. s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾加 ：watch → he

3. 辅音+y → ：study → she

4. 元音+y 只加 ：enjoy → she

5. have → he

6. Tom English aloud every morning.

7. He documentaries on weekends.

8. Linda chemistry before exams.

9. says 读 ；goes 读 。

第 6 页

否定句 don't / doesn't

课堂流程

  1. 对比 don't + 原形 与 doesn't + 原形。
  2. 牢记照妖镜：助动词已表三单，动词回原形。
  3. 双空操练：助动词 + 动词原形。

讲义填空

1. I play → I the piano.

2. We walk → We to school on rainy days.

3. Ella likes → Ella history.

4. He watches → He TV.

5. My sister studies → My sister English.

6. Teng Fei has → Teng Fei a fishing rod.

7. She goes → She to the library.

8. 照妖镜：doesn't 后动词用 ，不用三单。

9. 自造否定句：My brother computer games every day.

第 7 页

一般疑问句 Do / Does

课堂流程

  1. 火车头：Do/Does 提前，主语后动词用原形。
  2. 注意疑问句中人称代词小写。
  3. 双空操练至熟练。

讲义填空

1. You play the piano. → you the piano?

2. They live near the park. → they near the park?

3. She plays the erhu. → she the erhu well?

4. He goes to school by subway. → he to school by subway?

5. She has a sister. → she a sister?

6. Does she have…? 中 have 不用 。

7. Does she often help you? 中 often 在 与 之间。

8. Do you agree that reading widens our minds? → 句首助动词：

9. 自造疑问句： your parents tea after dinner?
