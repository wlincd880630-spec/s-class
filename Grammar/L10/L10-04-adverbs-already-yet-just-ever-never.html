标志词：already · yet · just · ever · never

位置 + 语气 + 常见错误（本页专讲五个「小信号灯」，不展开 for/since 与口语长对话。）

上一节我们区分了两大「意义」；这一节学习五个副词，让听者知道你是刚完成、已经完成、还没完成、曾经做过还是从未做过。

五个副词：位置口诀 + 例句

本课 already 只教常见位置：have/has 之后、过去分词之前，以降低混乱。

already

Often shows something happened sooner than expected — “already done”.

  • I've already read two books this summer.
  • She has already finished her summer homework.

口诀：已经 → 常放在 have/has 后面，再跟过去分词。

yet

In questions/negatives: “so far / still not” — usually at the end.

  • Have you chosen your club yet?
  • I haven't decided on my reading list yet.

口诀：疑问、否定里的「还/尚」→ 句末；肯定陈述表「已经」请用 already。

just

“A very short time ago” — between have/has and the past participle.

  • I've just finished my plan for the new term.
  • We've just come back from morning running.

口诀：刚刚 → 夹在 have 与 done 中间。

ever

In questions: “at any time before now?” — before the past participle phrase.

  • Have you ever tried an English corner?
  • Has he ever missed a team practice?

口诀：Have you ever …? → ever 紧跟在 you 后、过去分词前，朗读时可在 ever 后轻停顿。

never

“Not at any time” — between have/has and the past participle (like “not ever”).

  • I've never joined a school club before.
  • She has never skipped a volunteer day.

口诀：不要写成 don't never；用 have/has + never + done。

错句诊所（错 → 正）

色盲友好：红框旁标「误」，绿框旁标「正」，并附文字图标。

✗ 误

Have you finished yet your homework?

错因：yet 作「还/尚」讲时，应放在句末，不要插在宾语前。

✓ 正

Have you finished your homework yet?

✗ 误

I have finished already my homework.

错因：already 通常放在 have 后，不放在宾语后面。

✓ 正

I have already finished my homework.

✗ 误

I haven't finished my homework already.

错因：否定「还没」用 yet（句末），不用 already。

✓ 正

I haven't finished my homework yet.

✗ 误

Never I have seen such a thick grammar book.

错因：never 不能单独顶在句首接现完；应放在 have 与过去分词之间。

✓ 正

I have never seen such a thick grammar book.

迷你对照：already 与 yet

语气常见结构例句（教材风）
肯定：已经做了 have/has + already + p.p. We've already practised dialogue twice today.
否定：还没做 haven't/hasn't + … + yet（句末） I haven't handed in the report yet.
疑问：做了吗 / 有了吗 Have/Has + 主语 + … + yet? Has your group chosen a topic for the poster yet?

提醒：肯定陈述一般不用 yet 表示「已经」，避免说 ×I have finished yet.（除非极特殊语气，本课不作要求。）

小结

  • 五个副词像「信号灯」：告诉对方时间关系 + 预期（刚 / 已 / 尚 / 曾 / 从未）。
  • 位置题中考常考：yet 句末、already / just / never 常在 have 后、过去分词前、ever 在疑问里 have 后。
  • 初二阶段 already 与 just 分开练，避免同句堆砌造成超纲。
