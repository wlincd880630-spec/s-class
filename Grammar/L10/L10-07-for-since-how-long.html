多长时间了？— for & since

How long have you…?

上一节你用 already / yet 写了新学期准备；这一节抓「多久」——for + 时间段 与 since + 起点。

for 与 since 对照

类型 搭配与例句 中文提示
for for + 一段时间：for two years, for three months, for a week, for several days … 回答「有多久」，后面跟可量的长度。
since since + 起点：since 2019, since last summer, since Monday, since I was in Grade 7, since I joined the club … 回答「从什么时候起」，后面跟时间点 / 年份 / 从句（since 后可接从句）。

for six years 与 since six years ago 常可换思路表达，但 since + 纯时间段（如 since three years）不地道，应改为 for three years 或 since 2022 / since three years ago。

How long…? 问答范例（志愿与学习）
