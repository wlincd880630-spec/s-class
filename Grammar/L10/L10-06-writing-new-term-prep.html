写作：为新学期做准备

I have already… · I haven't … yet. · 至少五句

上一节我们练了口头 Have you ever…?；这一节把准备写进段落，用 already 与 yet 对照「已完成 / 尚未完成」。

写作任务

  • 写一段英语，至少五句，主题：为准备新学期你已经做了什么 / 还没做什么。
  • 文中须含至少两句带 already 的肯定（如 I have already… / We've already…）。
  • 文中须含至少两句带 yet 的否定（I haven't … yet. 或 have not … yet，yet 在句末）。
  • 鼓励再写一句用 just 或 because / so 表因果，与「意义 A：影响现在」呼应。
  • 建议两段式：先写「已做」，用 However, 或 But 转折，再写「未做」。

You will write a short paragraph in English about how you are getting ready for the new term. Your paragraph must have at least five sentences. It must include at least two sentences with already and at least two sentences with … yet at the end of the clause. Try to add one sentence with just or because / so.

词库

以下为「词块」与「例句」；点击后追加到顶栏「AI 点评草稿」打开面板中的英语草稿框，可自行连成完整句并调整大小写。

例句

教师范文（约 90 词）

高亮：already 句、yet 句、just。旁注：yet 放在否定句末尾，表示「到目前为止还没」。

The new term is coming soon. I have already planned my timetable for the first week, and I have already talked to my parents about pocket money. I've just finished cleaning my desk so that I can study there every evening. However, I haven't bought new notebooks yet, and I haven't decided which club to join yet. I feel a little nervous, but I feel more confident because I have already reviewed Unit 1 in the workbook.

小结

  • 用 already 突出「已经完成的准备」，用 … yet 诚实写出「还没做到的缺口」。
  • 写完后自查：have/has 一致、yet 在否定句末、连接词是否让两段衔接自然。
