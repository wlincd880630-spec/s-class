Relative Clauses 01

定语从句专项练习：Who vs Whom

Part 1: Combine the Sentences

使用 who 或 whom 将两个简单句合并为一个定语从句。

1. The scientist discovered the new element. He was awarded the Nobel Prize.
2. The volunteers helped clean up the beach. They were highly praised.
3. The candidates showed great potential. The company interviewed them yesterday.

Part 2: Fill in the Blanks

填入 who 或 whom。

1. Students ________ want to apply for the scholarship must submit applications before Friday.
2. The professor ________ we met at the academic conference is a famous historian.
3. Do you know the girl ________ won the first prize in the national English speech contest?
4. The children ________ the teacher encouraged are now very confident.

Part 3: Sentence Splitting

将下列句子拆分为两个独立的简单句。

1. People who are exposed to too much stress are more likely to suffer from health problems.

2. The passengers who survived the plane crash felt incredibly lucky.

Part 4: Multiple Choice

选择与英文句子最贴切的中文翻译。

1. I still remember the teacher who always encouraged me.
2. Anyone who breaks the law should be punished.
3. Employees who have worked for over ten years will get extra leave.
4. He is the kind of person who never gives up easily.

Part 5: 分层拓展 · B 级（宾语 whom）

用 who / whom 合并句子或填空。

1. The artist created this painting. The museum displayed the painting.
2. The coach praised the players. We trained with the players last summer.
3. The doctor ________ the villagers respect travels to mountain areas every month.

Part 6: 分层拓展 · C 级（whose）

填入 whose / who / whom / which。

1. The boy ________ sister won the math contest studies in our school.
2. Du Fu is a poet ________ poems are still widely read today.
3. The house ________ windows face south is warmer in winter.
