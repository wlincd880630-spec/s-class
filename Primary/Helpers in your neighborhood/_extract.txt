We live in different places.
Many people work in our neighborhoods.

(Hi, I’m Mateo. I live in Chile.)

(Hi, I’m Ava. I live in Angola.)

(Hi, I’m Emma. I live in the United States of America.)

(Hi, I’m Lucas. I live in Brazil.)

(Hi, I’m Kate. I live in the United Kingdom.)

Doctors and nurses help us get better.
People work hard in our neighborhoods.
They help us stay safe and happy.
