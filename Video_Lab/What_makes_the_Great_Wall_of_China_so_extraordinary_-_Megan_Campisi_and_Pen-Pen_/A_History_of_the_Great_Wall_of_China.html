概览 (Overview)

Early construction using rammed earth / 早期使用夯土技术建造的长城
Watchtowers used smoke signals for communication / 烽火台使用狼烟进行通信
The Manchu overthrow of the Ming Dynasty / 满族人推翻明朝
Today, the Great Wall welcomes millions of tourists / 如今，长城迎接数百万游客

The Great Wall of China wasn't built all at once. Its story begins during the Zhou dynasty, when individual states built rammed-earth walls for protection. After unifying China in 221 BCE, Emperor Qin Shi Huang connected and expanded these existing walls to create a more formidable defense against northern nomadic tribes. This massive undertaking marked the first major phase of the wall's construction, laying the foundation for what would become a legendary structure.

中国的长城并非一蹴而就。它的历史始于周朝的封建诸侯国，当时各国为防御而修筑了土墙。公元前221年，秦始皇统一中国后，将这些旧墙连接并扩建，以抵御北方游牧部落，形成了长城最初的规模。

Construction of the wall was a monumental task that came at a great human cost. Under the Qin and later Han dynasties, hundreds of thousands of people, including soldiers, forcibly conscripted peasants, and criminals, were put to work. The harsh conditions led to many deaths from accidents, hunger, and exhaustion. While legends speak of workers being buried within the wall itself, archaeologists have instead found many graves nearby, confirming the project's reputation as a place of immense suffering.

修建长城是一项艰巨的工程，也付出了巨大的人力代价。在秦汉两代，数十万士兵、被强征的农民和罪犯被迫劳动。严酷的环境导致许多人因事故、饥饿和劳累而死亡。虽然传说有工人被埋在墙内，但考古学家在附近发现了许多墓穴，证实了这项工程带来的巨大苦难。

Despite its scale, the wall was not invincible. Mongol forces, including those led by Genghis Khan, successfully breached it in the 13th century. In response, the later Ming Dynasty (1368-1644) undertook a massive reinforcement project. They used stronger materials like brick and stone, building the iconic structure we know today, complete with watchtowers for signaling. Even this improved wall was eventually overcome when Manchu clans established the Qing Dynasty, rendering the wall's defensive purpose obsolete.

尽管规模宏大，长城也并非坚不可摧。13世纪，蒙古军队就曾成功突破它。为此，后来的明朝（1368-1644）进行了大规模加固，使用砖石等更坚固的材料，并修建了用于传递信号的烽火台。但最终，满族人建立了清朝，使长城失去了防御作用。

After the Qing dynasty, the Great Wall fell into disrepair. However, it found new purpose in modern times. Today, it is no longer a military barrier but a powerful cultural symbol and a UNESCO World Heritage site that attracts millions of visitors. While it’s a myth that the wall is visible from the moon, its true significance lies on Earth, where it stands as a remarkable monument to human history, perseverance, and achievement.

清朝之后，长城逐渐失修。然而，它在现代找到了新的意义。如今，它不再是军事屏障，而是一个强大的文化象征和世界遗产，吸引着数百万游客。虽然从月球上看到长城的说法只是个传说，但它真正的价值在于地球本身，见证着人类的历史、毅力和成就。
