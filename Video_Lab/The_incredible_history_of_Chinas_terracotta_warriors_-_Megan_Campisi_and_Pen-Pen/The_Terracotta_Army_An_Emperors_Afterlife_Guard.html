概览 (Overview)

An army for the afterlife / 守护来世的军队
Each soldier is unique / 每位士兵都独一无二
Artisans at work / 劳作中的工匠
A tomb of legends / 传说中的陵墓

What happens after we die? While we can only guess, one ancient Chinese emperor had a clear plan. He wanted to continue his rule in the afterlife, and for that, he would need an army. In 1974, farmers in China accidentally discovered his secret: a massive underground army of over 8,000 life-size clay soldiers, all ready for battle. This incredible find revealed the ambition of China's first emperor, Qin Shi Huang.

人死后会发生什么？虽然我们只能猜测，但中国古代的一位皇帝却有着明确的计划。他希望在来世继续统治，为此他需要一支军队。1974年，中国农民偶然发现了他的秘密：一支庞大的地下军队，拥有超过八千名真人大小、整装待发的兵马俑。这一惊人发现揭示了中国第一位皇帝秦始皇的雄心。

Qin Shi Huang came to power at age 13 and later united China's warring kingdoms to become its first emperor. During his reign, he standardized writing, weights, and measures, and initiated the Great Wall. Despite his power, he was obsessed with his own mortality and searched for a way to live forever. He began constructing his enormous tomb complex, including the Terracotta Army, early in his reign to ensure his power would extend into the next world.

秦始皇13岁掌权，后来统一了战国，成为中国的第一位皇帝。他在位期间统一了文字、度量衡，并开始修建长城。尽管大权在握，他却对生死问题极为着迷，并寻求长生不老之法。他很早就开始建造自己巨大的陵墓，包括这支兵马俑军队，以确保他的权力能延续到来世。

The clay soldiers stand in battle formation across several pits. They are incredibly detailed, with each statue having unique facial features, hairstyles, and expressions, just like real warriors. Originally, they were painted in bright colors, but most of the paint has faded over time. Besides soldiers, the tomb complex also included figures of musicians, officials, and animals. This suggests the emperor planned for more than just war in his afterlife. Remarkably, he chose to use these models instead of sacrificing living people, a common practice in earlier times.

这些兵马俑在几个坑中列成战阵。它们的细节惊人，每尊雕像都有独特的面部特征、发型和表情，如同真人。起初，它们被涂上鲜艳的色彩，但随着时间流逝，大部分颜料已经褪去。除了士兵，陵墓中还有乐师、官员和动物的陶俑。这表明皇帝为来世做的准备远不止战争。值得注意的是，他选择使用陶俑，而非像前人那样用活人殉葬。
