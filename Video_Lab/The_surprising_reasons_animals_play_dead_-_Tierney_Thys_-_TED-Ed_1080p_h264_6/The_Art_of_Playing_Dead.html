概览 (Overview)

An opossum playing dead to survive. / 一只正在装死求生的负鼠。
Physiological changes during tonic immobility. / 强直静止状态下的生理变化。
A male spider feigns death to mate safely. / 一只雄性蜘蛛为安全交配而装死。
A researcher induces tonic immobility in a shark. / 一名研究员正在诱导鲨鱼进入强直静止状态。

When a dangerous animal attacks, the Virginia opossum often plays dead. Its body goes stiff. It drools and makes a bad smell. When the attacker leaves, the possum gets up and walks away. This trick is called tonic immobility. Scientists also write it as T I. Many other animals do something similar. Ants, lizards, chickens, and even sharks can stay very still or look dead so the enemy thinks they are not good food.

遇到危险时，弗吉尼亚负鼠常常会“装死”。身体会发硬，会流口水，还会发出难闻的气味。等攻击者离开后，负鼠再站起来走开。这种现象叫做强直静止，科学家也常把它写成 T I。很多别的动物也会做类似的事：蚂蚁、蜥蜴、鸡，甚至鲨鱼。它们一动不动或看起来像死了，好让敌人觉得不值得吃它们。

The animal does not plan this on purpose. Its body does it by itself. Part of the nervous system slows the heart and breathing. Scientists also think brain chemicals like dopamine may matter. Studies on beetles show that some play dead more often than others. Even when the body looks frozen, the animal may still notice what is happening and wait for a safe time to move.

动物并不是故意计划这样做的，是身体自己做出的反应。神经系统的一部分会让心跳和呼吸变慢。科学家认为大脑里的化学物质（例如多巴胺）也可能有关系。对甲虫的研究发现，有的个体更爱装死。身体看起来僵住的时候，动物可能仍在注意周围，等安全了再动。

Tonic immobility is not only for staying safe. Some fish lie on the bottom of the lake and look like dead meat. Small fish come to eat, and the big fish eats them. Some male spiders play dead so the female spider will not attack them while she eats a gift. But tonic immobility can also hurt animals. Killer whales have learned to turn great white sharks upside down. The sharks stay frozen so long that they cannot breathe and die.

强直静止不只是用来保护自己。有的鱼躺在湖底，看起来像死肉，引小鱼过来，大鱼再吃掉它们。有的雄蜘蛛会装死，好让雌蜘蛛吃“礼物”的时候不要攻击自己。但强直静止也会伤害动物：虎鲸会把大白鲨翻成肚皮朝上，鲨鱼僵住太久没法呼吸就会死掉。

People can freeze in a similar way when they are very scared or hurt. They cannot easily fight or run away. This is an old survival response, not a choice they make on purpose. Knowing this helps us understand why some people in danger do not act the way we expect. Learning from animals also teaches us about our own ways of staying alive.

人在非常害怕或受伤时，也可能类似地“僵住”，很难反抗或逃跑。这是一种很古老的生存反应，不是人们故意做出的选择。了解这一点，有助于我们理解为什么有些身处危险的人，表现可能和想象的不一样。从动物身上学习，也能让我们更懂人类自己的生存本能。
