概览 (Overview)

Left: The legend of Cangjie / 仓颉造字的传说; Right: Oracle bone script / 甲骨文
Character evolution: person, tree, and rest / 汉字演变：人、木、休
Radical (meaning) + Phonetic (sound) = Character / 形旁（表意）+ 声旁（表音）= 形声字
Traditional vs. Simplified character for 'dragon' / 繁体字与简体字“龙”

A legend tells of Cangjie, the Yellow Emperor's historian, creating the first Chinese characters after being inspired by nature. While this story is mythical, the earliest known Chinese writing dates back to the Shang Dynasty (around 1250-1050 BCE), inscribed on oracle bones. This makes it one of the world's oldest writing systems. These ancient characters were primarily pictograms, simple drawings that represented the objects they were meant to depict, like "人" for person and "木" for tree.

传说黄帝的史官仓颉受自然启发，创造了最早的汉字。虽然这只是神话，但已知的最早汉字可追溯到商代（约公元前1250-1050年）的甲骨文，是世界最古老的文字系统之一。这些古老的文字主要是象形文字，即描摹事物形状的简单图画，例如“人”字和“木”字。

As the language evolved, characters became more complex. Besides pictograms, there are ideograms representing abstract ideas (like numbers 二, 三) and compound ideograms combining symbols (like 人 'person' and 木 'tree' forming 休 'rest'). However, most modern characters are logograms, composed of two parts: a radical that suggests the meaning (e.g., the "water" radical in 河 'river') and a phonetic component that hints at the pronunciation. There are 214 main radicals that help categorize thousands of characters.

随着语言发展，汉字变得更加复杂。除了象形字，还有表示抽象概念的指事字（如数字“二”、“三”）和组合符号而成的会意字（如“人”和“木”组成“休”）。但多数现代汉字是形声字，由两部分组成：表示意义的偏旁部首（如“河”字里的“水”旁）和提示发音的声旁。214个主要部首帮助我们对数以千计的汉字进行归类。

While pronunciation varies greatly across China's dialects, the written language is standardized. Chinese grammar is also relatively straightforward, lacking gendered nouns or complex verb conjugations; tense can be shown by adding particles like "了" (le). The script has transformed over millennia, from bone carvings to brushed paper. In the 1950s, the government introduced simplified characters, now standard in mainland China, while traditional characters are still used in Hong Kong and Taiwan. This ancient system has also heavily influenced neighboring languages, notably Japanese.

尽管中国各地方言的发音差异巨大，但书面语是统一的。中文语法也相对直接，没有名词的性和复杂的动词变位，可通过助词“了”等来表示时态。汉字在数千年间不断演变，从骨刻到纸书。1950年代，政府推行了简体字，现为中国大陆的标准，而香港和台湾仍使用繁体字。这个古老的系统也深刻影响了日语等邻国语言。
