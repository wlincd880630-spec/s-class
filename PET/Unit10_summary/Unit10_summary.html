Unit Quiz
Page 1: Vocabulary Study
absolutely
🇺🇸 Completely; used to emphasize that something is completely true.
🇨🇳 绝对地；完全。
中考
You are absolutely right! The answer is C.
你绝对是对的！答案是C。
高一
The view from the top of the mountain was absolutely breathtaking, making the difficult climb worthwhile.
山顶的景色简直美极了，让这艰难的攀登变得物超所值。
according to
🇺🇸 As stated by or reported by someone or something.
🇨🇳 根据；按。
中考

按照计划，我们将会在周一参观博物馆。
高一

根据最近的研究，充足的睡眠对学业成绩至关重要。
admit
🇺🇸 To agree that something is true, especially unwillingly.
🇨🇳 承认；准许进。
中考

汤姆承认他不小心打破了窗户。
高一
She had to admit that her lack of preparation contributed to the project's failure.
她不得不承认，准备不足导致了项目的失败。
although
🇺🇸 Used to introduce a statement that makes the main statement surprising.
🇨🇳 虽然；尽。
中考

虽然他很年轻，但他对电脑了解很多。
高一
