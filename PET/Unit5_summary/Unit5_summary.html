Unit Quiz
Page 1: Vocabulary Study
access
🇺🇸 The right or opportunity to use or look at something.
🇨🇳 通道；进入；使用。
中考
Students can have free access to the school library.
学生可以免费使用学校图书馆。
高一
Many people in remote areas still do not have access to the internet.
许多偏远地区的人们仍然无法接入互联网。
actually
🇺🇸 Used to show that what is true is different from what you thought.
🇨🇳 实际上；事实。
中考
The math problem looked hard, but actually it was very easy.
这道数学题看起来很难，但实际上很容易。
高一
He didn't actually read the book; he just read a summary online.
他实际上并没有读这本书；他只是在网上读了一个摘要。
approach
🇺🇸 To move closer to someone or something; a way of dealing with something.
🇨🇳 接近；靠近；方法
中考

随着期末考试的临近，学生们学习更努力了。
高一

我们需要采取一种科学的方法来解决这个环境问题。
arrange
🇺🇸 To organize or make plans for something to happen.
🇨🇳 安排；筹备；整理
中考

你能把书架上的书整理一下吗。
高一

老师安排我们下周五去参观当地的博物馆。
as long as
🇺🇸 Used to say that something must happen before something else can happen; provided that.
🇨🇳 只要；既。
中考

只要你先完成作业，你就可以看电视。
高一

🇺🇸 To be present at an event, meeting, or function.
🇨🇳 出席，参加；照料

高一

🇨🇳 吸引，吸引力；名胜，景点
