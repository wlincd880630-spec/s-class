abundant
🇺🇸 Existing in large quantities; plentiful.
🇨🇳 大量的；丰富的；充裕的
中考

我们这里夏天阳光充足。
高一

该地区自然资源丰富，尤其是煤炭和石油。
avoid
🇺🇸 To keep away from or stop oneself from doing something.
🇨🇳 避免；避开；防止
中考
You should avoid playing computer games for too long.
你应该避免玩电脑游戏太久。
高一
He tried to avoid answering the difficult question by changing the topic.
他试图通过转移话题来避免回答这个难题。
