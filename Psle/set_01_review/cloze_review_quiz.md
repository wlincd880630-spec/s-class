## 一、基础巩固（多样化 · 20分）

### A. 固定搭配连线（5分）

将左列短语与右列中文释义配对（写 1-e 形式）。

| 左栏 | 右栏 |
|------|------|
| 1. shop for | a. 削价出售 |
| 2. on sale | b. 照顾 |
| 3. look after | c. 害怕 |
| 4. write with a pen | d. 用钢笔书写 |
| 5. be afraid of | e. 为……购物 |

### B. 选词填空（5分）

从方框选词填空，每词限用一次。

`for` · `on` · `size` · `thirsty` · `write` · `happily` · `looking at` · `choose` · `afraid` · `takes`

1. After playing basketball, he feels very ______.
2. My mother is ______ the new dress in the shop window.
3. It ______ her one hour to do her homework every day.
4. Please ______ your favourite colour.
5. ______ a letter to your pen friend.（祈使句）

### C. 中英短语互译（5分）

**英译中**

2. look for ________________
3. for sale ________________

**中译英**

### D. 判断正误并改错（5分）

正确写 T；错误写 F，并改正加粗部分。

1. I **spent** two hours to finish my homework yesterday.  
   (　) 改正：________________
2. The children are playing **happy** in the park.  
   (　) 改正：________________
3. These shoes are **on sale** for only 50 yuan.  
   (　) 改正：________________
4. Please **look at** your keys. I think you left them on the desk.  
   (　) 改正：________________
5. The book **costs** me 20 yuan.  
   (　) 改正：________________

## 二、提高训练（20分）

### A. 单项选择（每题 2.5 分，共 10 分）

1. — How much did you ______ for the new bike?  
   — 300 yuan.  
   A. take　B. spend　C. pay　D. cost

2. My little sister is ill. I have to ______ her at home.  
   A. look at　B. look for　C. look after　D. look like

3. It ______ the workers two years to build this bridge.  
   A. spent　B. took　C. paid　D. cost

4. The teacher was unhappy because the students were talking ______ in class.  
   A. happy　B. happily　C. noisy　D. noisily

### B. 短文填空（每空 2.5 分，共 10 分）

词框：`at` / `for` / `on` / `chooses` / `pay`

Mary wants to buy a gift for her friend’s birthday. She goes to a shop and looks (1) ______ many things. There is a nice cup (2) ______ sale. It is very cheap. She (3) ______ the blue one. Then she goes to the cashier to (4) ______ for it.

## 三、挑战拓展（10分）

### 微型完形填空（每空 2.5 分）

Lily wants a new schoolbag. She sees many beautiful bags (1) ______ sale in a store. She (2) ______ a pink one. Her mother (3) ______ for it. Then she says (4) ______, “You look great with the new bag!”

1. A. in　B. on　C. for　D. at  
2. A. looks　B. takes　C. chooses　D. pays  
3. A. spends　B. takes　C. costs　D. pays  
4. A. happy　B. happily　C. sad　D. sadly

## 四、参考答案与解析

### 一、基础巩固

**A.** 1-e　2-a　3-b　4-d　5-c

**B.** 1. thirsty　2. looking at　3. takes　4. choose　5. Write

**C.**  
1) 做某事花费某人多少时间  
2) 寻找  
3) 待售  
4) on sale  
5) choose a shirt

**D.**  
1. F — I **spent** two hours **doing** my homework yesterday.（或：It **took** me two hours to do…）  
2. F — playing **happily**  
3. T  
4. F — **look for** your keys  
5. T（物作主语 + cost 正确）

### 二、提高训练

**A.** 1.C（pay for）　2.C（look after）　3.B（It takes/took…）　4.D（副词 noisily）  
**B.** 1.at　2.on　3.chooses　4.pay

### 三、挑战拓展

1.B（on sale）　2.C（chooses）　3.D（pays for）　4.B（happily）
