# 第01套 · 词汇 · 词组 · 句型用法总结

> 不展示完形答案。每条含：用法说明 + 例句 + 造句练习 + 句子排序练习。

## 怎么用

1. 先读懂用法与例句  
2. 按中文提示用该词/词组/句型自己造一句  
3. 把打乱的单词排出正确句子  

---

## 1. shop for（词组）

**意思：** 为买……而购物  
**用法：** `shop for + 名词`

**例句：** Mum takes the boys to shop for clothes and school things.  
妈妈带男孩们去买衣服和文具。

**造句：** 用 shop for 写一句——妈妈周末去商场买水果。  
参考：Mum goes to the shopping centre to shop for fruit at the weekend.

**句子排序：** We / shop / for / new / shoes / before / school / starts / .  
→ We shop for new shoes before school starts.

---

## 2. It takes ... to do（句型）

**意思：** 做某事花费（某人）多少时间  
**用法：** `It takes (sb.) + 时间 + to do sth.`

**例句：** It doesn't take a long time for the kids to go here and there.  
孩子们用不了多久就会到处乱跑。

**造句：** 用 It takes ... to ... 写一句——我骑自行车到学校要十五分钟。  
参考：It takes me fifteen minutes to ride to school.

**句子排序：** It / takes / her / ten / minutes / to / walk / home / .  
→ It takes her ten minutes to walk home.

---

## 3. size（词汇）

**意思：** 尺寸；号码  
**用法：** What's your size? / I don't know my size.

**例句：** Jim would like a new shirt, but he doesn't know his size.  
吉姆想要一件新衬衫，但他不知道自己的尺码。

**造句：** 用 size 写一句——这双鞋对我来说尺码不对。  
参考：This pair of shoes is the wrong size for me.

**句子排序：** Do / you / know / your / shirt / size / ?  
→ Do you know your shirt size?

---

## 4. thirsty（词汇）

**意思：** 口渴的  
**用法：** be thirsty（口渴）；饿了用 hungry。

**例句：** Bob is thirsty, so he buys three bottles of cola.  
鲍勃口渴了，所以他买了三瓶可乐。

**造句：** 用 thirsty 写一句——跑步之后我很渴，想喝水。  
参考：After running, I am very thirsty and want some water.

**句子排序：** The / boys / are / thirsty / after / shopping / .  
→ The boys are thirsty after shopping.

---

## 5. on sale（词组）

**意思：** 打折；促销  
**用法：** on sale = 打折；for sale = 待售。

**例句：** The cola bottles are on sale today.  
这些可乐今天打折。

**造句：** 用 on sale 写一句——这些书包正在打折，很便宜。  
参考：These schoolbags are on sale and they are cheap.

**句子排序：** The / shoes / are / on / sale / this / week / .  
→ The shoes are on sale this week.

---

## 6. look at（词组）

**意思：** 看；注视  
**用法：** look at = 看眼前的东西；look for = 寻找。

**例句：** Bob is looking at a pair of shoes.  
鲍勃正在看一双鞋。

**造句：** 用 look at 写一句——请看黑板上的图片。  
参考：Please look at the picture on the blackboard.

**句子排序：** Please / look / at / the / map / carefully / .  
→ Please look at the map carefully.

---

## 7. happily（用法）

**意思：** 高兴地；开心地（副词）  
**用法：** happy（形）→ happily（副），修饰动词。

**例句：** Tom sees his classmate and talks with him happily.  
汤姆看见同学，开心地和他说话。

**造句：** 用 happily 写一句——孩子们在公园里开心地玩耍。  
参考：The children play happily in the park.

**句子排序：** They / sing / happily / at / the / party / .  
→ They sing happily at the party.

---

## 8. choose（词汇）

**意思：** 挑选；选择  
**用法：** choose A from B = 从 B 中选 A。

**例句：** Tom is choosing a schoolbag from different colours.  
汤姆正在从不同颜色中挑选书包。

**造句：** 用 choose 写一句——我想选一件蓝色的外套。  
参考：I want to choose a blue coat.

**句子排序：** She / will / choose / a / red / pencil / .  
→ She will choose a red pencil.

---

## 9. afraid（词汇）

**意思：** 害怕的；担心的  
**用法：** be afraid / be afraid of sth. / be afraid to do sth.

**例句：** Mum is afraid when she cannot find her boys.  
妈妈找不到孩子们时很害怕。

**造句：** 用 afraid 写一句——小女孩晚上一个人在家会害怕。  
参考：The little girl is afraid when she stays at home alone at night.

**句子排序：** Don't / be / afraid / of / speaking / English / .  
→ Don't be afraid of speaking English.

---

## 10. write（词汇）

**意思：** 写；书写  
**用法：** write 用笔写；say 用嘴说。

**例句：** Mum picks up a pen and writes a new rule.  
妈妈拿起笔，写下一条新规定。

**造句：** 用 write 写一句——请在本子上写下你的名字。  
参考：Please write your name in your notebook.

**句子排序：** Please / write / your / name / on / the / paper / .  
→ Please write your name on the paper.

---

## 11. pay ... for（词组）

**意思：** 为……付款  
**用法：** 人 + pay + 钱 + for + 物。

**例句：** Mum pays a lot of money for the school things.  
妈妈为这些文具付了很多钱。

**造句：** 用 pay ... for 写一句——他花了五十元买这本书。  
参考：He pays fifty yuan for this book.

**句子排序：** I / pay / twenty / yuan / for / the / bag / .  
→ I pay twenty yuan for the bag.

---

## 12. look for（词组）

**意思：** 寻找  
**用法：** 找不到时用 look for；眼前看着用 look at。

**例句：** Mum is looking for Tom in the shopping centre.  
妈妈在商场里找汤姆。

**造句：** 用 look for 写一句——我在找我的钥匙。  
参考：I am looking for my keys.

**句子排序：** They / are / looking / for / their / teacher / .  
→ They are looking for their teacher.
