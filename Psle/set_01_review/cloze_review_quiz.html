A. 固定搭配连线（5分）

在右侧下拉框中选择对应释义编号（a–e）。

  1. shop for
  2. on sale
  3. look after
  4. write with a pen
  5. be afraid of

  1. 削价出售
  2. 照顾
  3. 害怕
  4. 用钢笔书写
  5. 为……购物

答案：e（为……购物）
2. on sale →
答案：a（削价出售）
3. look after →
答案：b（照顾）
4. write with a pen →
答案：d（用钢笔书写）

答案：c（害怕）

D. 判断正误并改错（5分）

先选 T/F；若选 F，请在横线写出改正后的关键词或整句。

1. I spent two hours to finish my homework yesterday.
改正：
F。spent … doing / It took me … to do
2. The children are playing happy in the park.
改正：
F。playing happily
3. These shoes are on sale for only 50 yuan.
T。on sale 表示打折正确。
4. Please look at your keys. I think you left them on the desk.
改正：
F。应改 look for（寻找）
5. The book costs me 20 yuan.
T。物作主语 + cost 正确。

A. 单项选择（每题 2.5 分）

1. — How much did you ______ for the new bike? — 300 yuan.
C。pay … for sth.
2. My little sister is ill. I have to ______ her at home.
C。look after 照顾
3. It ______ the workers two years to build this bridge.
B。It took sb. time to do sth.
4. The teacher was unhappy because the students were talking ______ in class.
