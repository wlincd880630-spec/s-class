The Mid-Autumn Festival is on August 15th of the lunar calendar. It's an important festival for family reunion. On that day, my family always has a big dinner together. After dinner, we sit in our garden, eating mooncakes and fruits. We admire the bright full moon and share interesting stories. I love spending this happy time with my family.
