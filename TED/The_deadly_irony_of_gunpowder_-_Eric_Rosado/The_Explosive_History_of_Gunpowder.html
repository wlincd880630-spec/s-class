概览 (Overview)

An alchemist accidentally discovers gunpowder / 一位炼金术士意外发现了火药
Gunpowder technology spreads west / 火药技术向西传播
Uncontained powder burns; contained powder explodes / 开放的火药会燃烧；密闭的火药会爆炸
From cannons to celebrations / 从大炮到庆典

The bright lights and loud booms of fireworks are all thanks to an ancient invention: gunpowder. This story begins in 9th-century China, where alchemists were trying to create a potion for eternal life. Instead, they accidentally created a flammable black powder. At first, they only used this “fire medicine” to create simple weapons like flaming arrows. It was only later that they figured out the right mix to make it explode, leading to the creation of both bombs and early fireworks, which were used to scare away evil spirits. This powerful invention did not stay in China for long. The knowledge of gunpowder spread across the world, likely carried by Mongol armies to Persia, India, and eventually to Europe around the 13th century.

Once gunpowder arrived in Europe, it was developed into powerful weapons like guns and cannons, changing warfare forever. But how does it propel a beautiful firework into the sky? The science is about pressure. A trail of gunpowder in the open air will simply burn, because the energy and gas can easily escape. However, inside a firework canister, the gunpowder is tightly packed. When it is ignited, the pressure builds up with nowhere to go. This contained energy then explodes, launching the firework upwards. A second fuse triggers another explosion high in the air, creating the colorful patterns we enjoy. So while the alchemists never found eternal life, they discovered something that has shaped human history through both destruction and celebration.
