Overview

An anxious person stands in a dimly lit, mystical room. On a central ornate table, a deck of tarot cards is spread out, glowing faintly. A shadowy, ethereal figure of Fate waits patiently on the other side.
An overhead view of a card game in progress on an ancient wooden table. A hand reaches for a tarot card labeled 'The Tower (16)'. Arrows show smaller cards labeled 1, 2, 4, and 8 moving to the opponent's side of the table.
A person intently watches a grainy, flickering video on an old camera's small screen. The footage shows a man, their father, looking defeated while playing a card game against a ghostly figure. The viewer has a look of sudden, sharp realization.
A first-person view of a hand hovering over a spread of beautifully illustrated tarot cards. Three cards glow brighter than the rest: 'The World (2)' depicting breaking chains, 'The Lovers (6)' depicting a couple, and 'The Wheel of Fortune (10)' depicting gold coins. The choice is about to be made.

Legend says your ancestor stole a magical tarot deck from Fate herself, making your family the world's greatest fortune-tellers. But this gift came with a curse: every 23 years, one family member must enter a duel with Fate, governed by arcane rules. Generation after generation, the outcome is the same—Fate claims their soul. Twenty-three years ago, your father faced the challenge and lost. Today, it's your turn.

While pacing the dueling room, you discover a hidden camera your father left behind. The tape reveals his game against Fate. The 22 cards of the Major Arcana, plus an additional card called the Soul, are laid out. Fate explains the rules: "You may take any card that has at least one factor on the table. I'll then receive every factor." For example, taking the Tower (16) gives Fate the cards for 1, 2, 4, and 8. The player with the highest total card value at the end wins.

During the game, Fate mentions that certain cards grant special rewards: the 10 brings great fortune, the 6 grants true love, and the 2 would end the family curse forever. However, your father's total comes up short, and he loses. Just as the video cuts to static, Fate appears before you in the room. She offers you the same starting card as your father: the Tower. The game begins.

You realize that accepting the Tower (16) is a trap. It gives Fate the 1, 2, 4, and 8 cards, which allows her to eventually claim all the prime-numbered cards. A careful calculation shows that this path leads, at best, to a 138-138 tie. Since ties go to Fate, this guarantees your loss. Fate has been manipulating the game from the first move to ensure her victory.

Thanks to your father's recording, you know better. To win, you must decline the Tower. To break the curse, you must take the 2 on your first turn, as it is a prime number. By carefully selecting a sequence of cards (like 22, 14, 21, and 15), you can achieve a narrow victory of 140 to 136. Alternatively, different opening moves could allow you to claim the 10 for wealth or the 6 for love, but you can only choose one reward. Fate is waiting for your decision.
